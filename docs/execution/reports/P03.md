## Implemented behavior

Preparations retain exact before/proposed Markdown and binary bytes under a hashed immutable manifest; sorted operations support guarded create/replace/delete and rename as create then delete. Source-head writes require revision assets first. Immutable record and source-revision protection does not depend on the current head existing. Identical origin lookups happen before allocation and reuse terminal outcomes. Preparation is read-only when planning, and actual preparation cannot touch canonical targets.

Length/header/body-protected journal frames use strict bounded JSON, checksums, sequences, binding and legal transitions. Durable state precedes dependent work; only incomplete final frames are ignored and synced truncation precedes new append. Applying rechecks all old/new/absent states, records intent, stages/syncs, guards replacement, syncs directories, and records completion. Recovery independently examines every target, replays old bytes despite completion flags, syncs observed-new bytes, and preserves third hashes as conflicts. Incomplete preparations without any authority artifact remain diagnostic retained orphans. Lost intent cannot be inferred from target bytes.

Trusted graph validation sees the whole canonical scan and all proposals, including binary overlays; a retained original baseline also binds dependencies outside that scan. Publication receives a private fully-applied permit under the real writer lock and is blocked by another incomplete apply. Terminal receipts retain the verified journal transcript, manifest/snapshot binding and guarded bookkeeping hashes. Terminal historical changes do not re-read obsolete targets. Explicit abort is safe for a known Prepared change after its target scope changes; journal loss still requires contained all-old targets. Guarded inverse proposals retain immutable captures and refuse unfamiliar edits.

Revision ownership is an engine policy: a checksummed claim binds the exact manifest, complete member hashes and original validation baseline. Known prepared changes cannot extend a now-sealed tree. Interrupted owners can resume only their own members and bounded private stage artifacts; additional assets, missing/misbound authority, and competing owners are preserved/refused. Namespace words use full Unicode folding consistently in inference, scanning and validation; IDs stay case-sensitive.
