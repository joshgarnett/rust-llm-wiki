# Representative public 1k workflow observations

The bounded public-import observations are complete. A disposable vault contains
1,000 captured Sources / 100,302,609 input bytes, 1,000 authored Pages, 32 Entities
and 16 Assertions. Inputs comprise varied synthetic UTF-8 procedures and two
licensed Cargo 0.85 documents; this is operational coverage, not realistic answer
quality or 25k qualification. Private expected facts and unseen HIGH questions
were not indexed or inspected.

The observed route used public capture/import, normalized activation, Page batches,
verified lexical search, exact source reads, refresh/withdrawal, complete backup,
cache-only removal and offline rebuild. Sixteen bootstrap captures plus 984 managed
items in 123 groups completed. Recovery preserved acknowledged and eight pending
Source/Revision identities and capture timestamps. All original/content payloads,
authored Pages, source/revision membership and frozen graph identities matched.
Ten refreshes produced immutable successors; withdrawal and unchanged refresh
returned the expected eligibility and no-op behavior.

All 66 planned operational queries completed: ten selected searches and ten mapped
reads plus two bounded Cargo contexts in each initial, updated and rebuilt state.
All observed supervised command intervals were at most 0.166 seconds. These are
single diagnostic observations with polling overhead and uncontrolled host activity,
not a latency distribution or answer-completeness score. Cargo clipping was declared
prospectively and remains visible; a correct excerpt does not establish a complete
answer. The rebuilt 12 substantive result packets matched the updated packets,
including ranking, read bytes/ranges, eligibility, context and exact citations.

Offline normalized reconstruction took 10.149 seconds; the subsequent complete
check took 13.820 seconds and returned no diagnostics. Complete backup bytes were
verified before cache removal. Across 14,358 inventory entries, only publication
bookkeeping, its parent directory times and diagnostic writer-lock PID text changed
through reconstruction. Source, retained-history and other substantive bytes,
mtimes and membership were preserved. The final queries changed no inventory entry.
