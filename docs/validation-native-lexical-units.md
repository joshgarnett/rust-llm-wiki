# Native lexical evidence units: rejected experiment

The query-ranked lexical-unit candidate is **NO_GO and unpromoted**. Retain the original baseline. Independent development review found 14/32 complete positive tasks against baseline 13/32; the declared gate required at least 17/32 and zero loss of baseline-supported facts. Both conditions failed.

The candidate constructs complete structural parents when they fit, explicitly clips oversized windows, ranks units with positive-IDF BM25, retains up to 32 per admitted owner and tries them in descending score order. Exact packing re-realizes original accepted membership and preserves citation authority on rejection. Route guards preserve fallback behavior and other modes. Construction, ranking, retention and packing changed together; this experiment cannot isolate BM25's causal contribution.

Inputs were 994 captured real documents, 5,582,095 UTF-8 bytes, from a 1,000-document WixQA/ConditionalQA/QASPER selection (750/150/100). Six withheld additions were never imported; the 24-question independent holdout remains sealed. The 40 unchanged development questions contain 32 positive tasks and eight absence controls. Paired evaluation retained identical immutable Source revisions, queries, caches, offline lexical defaults, proof limits and 12,000-byte/3,000-estimated-token final budgets. No provider API was called; credential references were not credentials used by this experiment.
