# Complete reading of small discovered Sources

The bounded whole-Source reading route passes its independently reviewed
development evidence gate: **5/5 tasks, all 14 required facts, 10/10 and zero
correctness blockers**. Ordinary context remains at 3/5 tasks and 11/14 facts.
The gain belongs to the assisted reading route; native retrieval is unchanged.

The [answer recipe](../skills/llm-wiki/references/cited-page.md#read-small-discovered-sources-completely)
now offers complete reads when discovered Sources are small enough for the user's
allowance. This addresses a measured failure: correct owners were discovered,
but their early examples displaced later explanations of defaults and control
variables. Earlier excerpt searches and bounded forward reads did not complete
all five tasks. The two captured files together occupy only 73,091 bytes.

current Source revisions, parser and generation2502 publication. The ordinary
context responses and initial verified discovery are reused with full provenance;
no question, threshold or exclusion changed.

Whole responses supply the missing timeout/default/control-variable and retry

## Measured bounds and limits

Full inherited context/discovery input totals 150,896 bytes. New stdout/stderr
totals 346,230 bytes, including wrappers and repeated reads; body text alone totals
320,591 bytes. Maximum per-task raw input is 116,137 bytes, below the frozen
131,072-byte allowance. No truncation, overflow, failed task or exclusion occurred.
This allowance is an evaluated example, not a new CLI default or a guarantee that
other files fit. Larger Sources still need bounded section or continuation reads.

The collector's process-owned monotonic interval is 192.102 seconds, including
the experimental full preservation audits. Recorded native command intervals sum
to 0.195 seconds, with a maximum of 0.039 seconds. These intervals are distinct;
the native sum is not end-to-end answer latency. The warm, fixed-order observations
do not establish cold performance, a distribution or capacity. Supervisor peak
RSS is 302.92 MB; native peak is 17.24 MB, with sampling limitations retained.

Initial/final full byte pins and all 92,514 canonical members remain unchanged.
The read response does not serialize a full snapshot: the independently reviewed
experimental binding uses exact database/WAL equality to an already accepted
publication, unchanged parser/authority/selectors and a serial no-writer interval.
The complete post-summary account is 10.190 GB allocated, including retained
earlier evidence; the aggregate ledger and free-space floor passed. A 0.00195-second
summary-write tail is measured separately; its own emission and exit are unmeasured.

The host launch used relative spellings for three launcher inputs whose resolved
files matched the absolute freeze. This was disclosed and independently checked;
the eight native command arrays exactly matched their freeze. The original failed
source review and previous failed search/read controls remain failed evidence.
